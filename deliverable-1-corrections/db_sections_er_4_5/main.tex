\subsection{Όψεις}

1) Όψη για τον αριθμό \selectlanguage{english}crypto\selectlanguage{greek} των χρηστών

\[
\textbf{\selectlanguage{english}UserCryptoCount} =
\pi_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}user\_id, name, ticker\_symbol, crypto\_amount}}
\Bigl(
   \bigl(
      \text{\selectlanguage{english}User} 
      
      \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(user\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}owns} \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(wallet\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}Wallet}
   \bigr)
   \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(wallet\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}has}
   \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(balance\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}CryptoBalance}
   \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(crypto\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}Cryptocurrency}
\Bigr)
\]

\vspace{1cm}

2) Όψη για τις συναλλαγές \selectlanguage{english}crypto\selectlanguage{greek} των χρηστών

\[
\textbf{\selectlanguage{english}UserCryptoTransactions} =
\pi_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}user\_id, name, wallet\_id, transaction\_hash, transaction\_type, amount, timestamp, ticker\_symbol}}

\Bigl(
   \bigl(
      \text{\selectlanguage{english}User} \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(user\_id)}}
      \text{\selectlanguage{english}owns} \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(wallet\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}Wallet}
   \bigr)
   \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(wallet\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}transacts}
   \bowtie_{\text{\selectlanguage{english}(crypto\_id)}} \text{\selectlanguage{english}Cryptocurrency}
\Bigr)
\]


\vspace{1cm}

3) Όψη για τα \selectlanguage{english}candlestick \selectlanguage{greek}των \selectlanguage{english}crypto 

\[
\textbf{CryptoCandlestick} =
\pi_{\text{ticker\_symbol, name, candle\_id, interval, open\_price,
high\_price, low\_price, close\_price, volume}}

\Bigl(
   \text{Cryptocurrency}
   \bowtie_{\text{(crypto\_id)}}
   \text{has}
   \bowtie_{\text{(candle\_id)}}
   \text{Candlestick}
\Bigr)
\]
